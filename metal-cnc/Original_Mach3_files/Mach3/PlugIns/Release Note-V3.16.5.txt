
Release Notes for XHC MACH3 Ethernet Motion Card

XHC NcEther.dll is the Driver for XHC Ethernet CARD,Used  for MACH3 cnc control system. 
You could control your cnc machine through our Ethernet card.
the card has these feature:
Standard ehternet  Interface
4/6 Axis Step/Direction Output
8 General Purpose Outputs 
16 General Purpose Inputs
1 PWM Output for Spindle Speed Control
Up to 2000Khz Steps pulse
