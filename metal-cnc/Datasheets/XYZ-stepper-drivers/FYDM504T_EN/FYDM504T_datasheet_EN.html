Product Features

  • High performance, high efficiency: the latest 32-bit DSP control technology, so the motor reaches its best output performance.
  • Low vibration, low noise: automatically calculates the resonance point and effectively suppresses the motor's noise and vibration at low running speeds.
  • Small temperature rise, long life: precision current control keeps the motor's temperature rise low.
  • Wide speed range: with a suitable stepper motor, up to 1500 rpm.
  • Interference frequency filtering: optically isolated differential signal input; pulse response frequency up to 250 Kpps.
  • Powerful, low repair rate: uses imported industrial-grade components; mature and stable technology; fault-status indication and automatic protection for undervoltage, overvoltage, overcurrent, short circuit, open circuit, phase error, etc.
  • Compatible stepper motors: 56, 60, 86 mm series.

Application Fields

Suitable for automation equipment and instruments in all kinds of motion-control fields, for example: electronic machining and inspection, semiconductor packaging, laser cutting and welding, laser phototypesetting, packaging machinery, engraving machines, marking machines, cutting machines, garment plotters, CNC machine tools and automated assembly equipment. It is the first choice for users in fields where low noise, superior high-speed performance and strong price/performance competitiveness are expected.

Note
  1. The input voltage must not exceed DC50V / AC40V. When the driver is powered up, the green PWR indicator lights.
  2. If the driver's fault indicator lights, check the motor wiring and the control signal lines. If it is a motor-wiring or other short-circuit fault, it must be powered off and on again to recover after the fault is cleared.
  3. SW4 sets the holding-current function: ON = Full Current, OFF = Half Current. SW4 is set to OFF at the factory, which reduces heat generation in the motor and driver and improves reliability. The current is automatically reduced by 50 % 100 ms after the pulse train stops.
  4. This driver has a single/double pulse selection function, settable through the communication port; the factory default is pulse + direction.
